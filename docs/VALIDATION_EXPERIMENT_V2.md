# Prospective validation target v2

The experiment verifies the pre-frozen oracle optimum. It is not used to choose the formulation.

## Frozen formulation, 100-part polyol basis

- PPG400: 40.000 parts
- PPG2000: 60.000 parts
- 4,4'-MDI: 55.30525 nominal parts
- nominal NCO:OH: 1.700

Final MDI mass must be recalculated from actual COA hydroxyl numbers and actual MDI NCO content while preserving NCO:OH=1.700.

## Preregistered preferred targets

- eta80: 2.2-5.5 Pa.s; ideal center 3.4785 Pa.s
- eta120: 0.30-0.60 Pa.s; ideal center 0.4243 Pa.s
- eta80/eta120: 7.0-9.5; ideal center 8.1548

Oracle point target: eta80=3.4427 Pa.s; eta120=0.4098 Pa.s; ratio=8.4016.

Broad functional zones are eta80=0.85-10 Pa.s, eta120=0.16-0.80 Pa.s and ratio=6-13.

Use at least 3 independent synthesis batches. Technical viscosity repeats quantify instrument repeatability but do not replace synthesis replication.
